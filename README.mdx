# KASA

## Insurance licensing

Insurance licensing requirements depend on the jurisdiction and the type of insurance you plan to sell. Confirm the requirements with the regulator where you intend to operate before applying.

Common prerequisites may include:

- Selecting the jurisdiction and line(s) of authority you need.
- Completing any required pre-licensing education.
- Passing the required licensing examination.
- Submitting an application and fee to the relevant regulator.
- Completing any required identity verification or background checks.

Requirements and steps vary. In the United States, start with your state insurance department or the [National Insurance Producer Registry](https://nipr.com/). Outside the United States, contact the applicable government insurance regulator.

This project does not issue insurance licenses. The regulator's current guidance is authoritative.

## Content licensing

Obtain permission from the copyright holder before using protected material. A licensing request should describe the material, intended use, territory, duration, distribution channels, and any modifications. Keep the written agreement and follow its terms.

This documentation does not provide links to unauthorized copies or torrent sites. Use authorized sources and obtain the required rights.
